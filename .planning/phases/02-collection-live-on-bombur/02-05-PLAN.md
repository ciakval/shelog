<objective>
Give the owner a phone push whenever collection stops being healthy, and a recovery push when it heals (OPS-05). Add an external dead-man's switch (ROB-01, pulled forward by D-12) and the poller's daily backup (OPS-04, D-14). All of it runs as housekeeping after each poll cycle and can never disturb polling.

Purpose: alerts sent by the poller itself cannot report a dead VPS, hence the healthchecks.io ping. Alerts must be loud for auth problems and quiet otherwise (transitions only).
Output: notify.py, alerts.py, housekeeping.py, the poller hook, `shelog alert-drill`, alert/ping status in `shelog check`, and tests including the forced auth-rejected drill.
</objective>
